Privacy Policy

Gali Cricket Chase 3D (Android package com.galicricket.chase3d)
Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains, in plain words, what information the game Gali Cricket Chase 3D ("the game", "we", "us") uses and why. We built the game so that it needs as little data as possible.

1. Your game on your phone

There is no sign-up and no password. Your progress (levels, coins, kits, bats, gear, look, Season Pass, settings and the names you type) is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may include this game data in your Google account backup (you can turn that off in your phone's backup settings).

2. Online Duel and leaderboard (only if you use them)

If you open Online Duel, the game signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication. This creates a random player ID; it is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account. To run duels and the leaderboard, the game then stores on Google Cloud Firestore (servers in Mumbai, India):

  • the player name and optional team name you choose (other players can see them);
  • your rating, rank, wins, duels played and best score (shown on the leaderboard);
  • your finished duel innings (the 12-ball seed, runs and wickets), so other players can play against your score;
  • results of other players who played against your score (who, their score and the rating change), which are removed once your game has read them.

Names are checked against a list of insults before they are used. If you never open Online Duel, nothing of this is created.

Delete it at any time: Online Duel > Delete my online data removes your profile, your innings and your results from the server and forgets the anonymous ID on your phone. You can also email us (section 12) and we will delete it.

3. Game analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To see which parts of the game are played, and to find and fix crashes (especially on low-end phones), the Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They may collect:

  • app activity: screens and game events such as a match ended (mode, runs, win/loss), a level finished, a duel played, an item bought with coins or a purchase completed;
  • an app-instance ID and, where allowed, the Advertising ID;
  • device and app information (model, Android and WebView version, language, app version, approximate country from the IP address);
  • crash logs and error messages from the game, with the graphics setting and the phone's graphics chip name.

This data is processed by Google on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms. Analytics event data is kept for 14 months.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's Advertising ID (Android advertising identifier);
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate location (city/country level);
  • device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewarded ads completed);
  • crash and diagnostic information related to ads.

This data is collected and shared directly with Google under Google's own policies. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, coin packs and the Season Pass) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether a purchase was successful so it can unlock the item, and the game can restore items that last (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Coin Doubler). Coin packs and the Season Pass are used up when bought: a Season Pass covers the season it was bought in. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Sharing a challenge or scorecard

If you tap Share, the game creates a short text (and, for the scorecard, an image) with your score and a challenge code and passes it to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp) using Android's share sheet. We do not receive it.

After a good match the game may ask Google Play to show its rating card (Google Play In-App Review). Google decides whether it appears, and your rating goes to Google Play, not to us.

7. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade any personal information. We do not collect contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone, camera or precise location.

8. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet / network state - for ads, purchases, Online Duel, analytics and crash reports.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) - used by AdMob for ads and by Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Billing - for Google Play in-app purchases.
  • Vibrate - for haptic feedback during play.

9. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your phone: until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Online Duel data: until you delete it in the game (Online Duel > Delete my online data) or ask us to (see Delete your data).
  • Analytics event data: 14 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Ads and purchases data held by Google: under Google's policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com.

11. Delete your data

You can have your Gali Cricket Chase 3D data deleted at any time:

  • In the game: Online Duel > Delete my online data. This removes your player profile (name, team, rating, wins, best score), your saved innings and the results linked to them from our server straight away, and forgets the anonymous player ID on your phone.
  • Without the game (for example, if you uninstalled it): email galigamesstudio@outlook.com with the subject "Delete my data - Gali Cricket Chase 3D" and the player name you used in Online Duel. We delete the matching Online Duel data within 30 days and reply when it is done.
  • Data on your phone: uninstall the game or clear its app data in Android settings.

Analytics and crash data are not linked to your name and are deleted automatically after the periods in section 10. Ads, purchases and Advertising ID data are held by Google; you can manage or delete them in your Google account and Android settings.

12. Security

All data the game sends (Online Duel, analytics, crash reports, ads and purchases) travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Server rules allow each player to change only their own Online Duel data.

13. Contact

Questions, or a request to delete your data: galigamesstudio@outlook.com

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
